For Accurate Readings

How to Take Photos of Your Palm

To clearly observe fine line nuances and mount contours, please follow these 4 guidelines:

☀️

Natural Daylight

Take photos near a window or outdoors during the day. Avoid harsh artificial night shadows.

🖐️

Both Palms Relaxed

Keep your palms flat and naturally relaxed. Do not over-stretch or cup your fingers.

📸

Crisp Close-Up Focus

Hold the camera steady close to your palm so delicate subsidiary lines remain sharp.

🚫

No Filters or Harsh Flash

Avoid camera beauty filters or direct flash, as they alter the genuine line texture and skin tone.
